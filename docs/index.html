A clear session model

Session phases and timer transitions live in a Kotlin engine, separate from the Compose screens. That makes pause, rest, skip, and completion behavior directly testable.

Explore the session engine ↗

Continuity beyond the screen

A session controller and Android foreground service coordinate active sessions, notification controls, and timing as the app moves into the background.

See background session handling ↗
✳

Personal by design

Compose brings together customizable routine cards, multiple color themes, and a liquid shader theme with a gradient fallback on older Android versions.
